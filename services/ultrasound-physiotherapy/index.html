Ultrasound Physiotherapy

Therapeutic ultrasound delivers deep, gentle heat to soft tissue, reducing pain and inflammation and speeding up healing in tendons, ligaments and muscle.

Gentle therapeutic ultrasound to calm pain and speed healing in stubborn soft-tissue injuries, at our Kakkanad clinic.

Some injuries sit too deep for heat packs and massage to reach. A strained tendon, an inflamed ligament or a stiff, irritated joint can stay sore for weeks, slowing your recovery and keeping you from the activities you enjoy. Therapeutic ultrasound is a gentle, drug-free physiotherapy technique that delivers deep, targeted warmth right into that tissue, easing pain and helping the body heal.

At Proud Physio & Wellness, therapeutic ultrasound is used by Dr. Noora Ameen TA (PT) as part of a wider treatment plan. A handheld probe glides over the skin with a thin layer of gel, sending high-frequency sound waves into the soft tissue below. You feel nothing more than a little warmth, yet beneath the surface it increases circulation, relaxes tight tissue and supports the natural repair process.

Conditions We Help With

Therapeutic ultrasound is most useful for soft-tissue injuries and stubborn inflammation where deep, controlled warmth helps recovery. We commonly use it for:

Tendinitis & tendinopathy Bursitis Ligament sprains Muscle strains Frozen shoulder Tennis & golfer's elbow Plantar fasciitis Joint stiffness Post-injury scar tissue

Because ultrasound relieves symptoms but does not fix the underlying cause on its own, we always pair it with the right exercises and hands-on care. Calming the tissue makes movement more comfortable, and that comfortable movement is what drives true, lasting recovery.

How Therapeutic Ultrasound Works

The probe produces high-frequency sound waves that pass harmlessly through the skin and are absorbed by the tissue beneath. This creates two helpful effects. The first is a gentle deep heating that increases blood flow, relaxes tight tissue and improves the flexibility of tendons and ligaments. The second is a subtle micro-vibration of the cells, which is thought to stimulate the natural healing response and help reduce swelling. Together, these prepare the injured area to respond better to exercise and manual therapy.

Ultrasound is not a cure on its own. It is a way to calm pain and prime healing so the exercises that truly restore you can do their work.

What a Session Feels Like

Therapeutic ultrasound is one of the most comfortable treatments in physiotherapy. A cool gel is applied to the skin, then the smooth head of the probe is moved slowly in small circles over the area for a few minutes. Most people feel only a mild, pleasant warmth, and some feel nothing at all. There are no needles, no medication and no recovery time, so you can carry on with your day immediately afterwards.

Why the gel? Sound waves do not travel well through air. The gel creates a smooth contact between the probe and your skin so the ultrasound passes efficiently into the tissue, exactly where it is needed.

What to Expect in a Session

  1. Assessment

    We examine the injured area, confirm the problem and check that ultrasound is a safe and suitable choice for you.

  2. Application

    Gel is applied and the probe is moved gently over the area for a few minutes, delivering controlled deep warmth to the tissue.

  3. Hands-on & exercise

    We combine ultrasound with manual therapy and targeted exercises, so the tissue that has been warmed and relaxed can move and strengthen.

  4. Home programme

    You leave with a simple plan of stretches and exercises to keep recovery moving between sessions.

Is Ultrasound Therapy Safe?

Therapeutic ultrasound is a safe, well-established and painless treatment when applied by a trained physiotherapist. Because it produces deep heat, we avoid certain areas and situations, such as directly over the abdomen during pregnancy, over active infections or cancers, over pacemakers, or on areas with reduced sensation. That is exactly why every session begins with a proper assessment. Where ultrasound is not the best fit, we choose an alternative such as myofascial release therapy or taping and cupping instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ultrasound therapy hurt?

No. Most people feel only a mild, pleasant warmth as the probe moves over the skin, and some feel nothing at all. There are no needles and no medication involved.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the injury. Ultrasound is usually one part of a short course of physiotherapy rather than a one-off treatment. After your assessment we will give you a clear, realistic plan.

Is ultrasound therapy the same as an ultrasound scan?

No. A diagnostic scan produces images of the inside of the body. Therapeutic ultrasound uses sound waves to deliver deep heat to injured tissue to relieve pain and support healing. They use similar technology for very different purposes.

Can ultrasound therapy be used on its own?

It is most effective as part of a plan. Ultrasound calms pain and inflammation, which makes the exercises and hands-on treatment that truly restore function easier and more comfortable to do.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Ultrasound is avoided over the abdomen and lower back during pregnancy, and in a few other situations. This is exactly why we assess carefully first and choose the safest, most effective option for you.
