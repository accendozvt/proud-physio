About the Role

We are looking for a male physiotherapist to join our team at Proud Physio & Wellness, Kakkanad. You will work alongside our lead physiotherapist, Dr. Noora Ameen TA (PT), assessing and treating patients of all ages with evidence-based, hands-on care, and helping each one recover fully and stay well.

What You Will Do

  • Assess patients and plan treatment for musculoskeletal, sports, neurological and post-surgical conditions
  • Deliver exercise therapy and manual therapy, along with electrotherapy such as TECAR and therapeutic ultrasound
  • Treat patients at the clinic and, when needed, on home visits
  • Teach patients and families their home exercises and how to keep pain from coming back
  • Keep clear treatment notes and track each patient's progress
