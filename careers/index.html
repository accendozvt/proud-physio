Careers at Proud Physio

Help people across Kochi move better, recover faster and live without pain. Join a small, caring team at our clinic in Kakkanad.

At Proud Physio & Wellness we treat every patient as a person, not a diagnosis. Led by Dr. Noora Ameen TA (PT), our team brings evidence-based physiotherapy to people of all ages, at the clinic, at home and online. If you share that care and want to grow as a clinician, we would love to hear from you.

Why Work With Us

Modern equipment

Work with TECAR therapy, therapeutic ultrasound, dry needling, taping and cupping alongside hands-on care.

Learn as you treat

Work closely with an experienced lead physiotherapist and keep building your clinical skills.

A varied caseload

Sports injuries, back and neck pain, neuro rehab, post-surgical recovery, women's health and children.

Patient-first care

Time to assess properly, explain clearly and see your patients through to a full recovery.
